Our Heritage

A Quarter Century
of La Dolce Vita

"Food is not merely sustenance. It is memory, love, and the passage of time made edible."

Founded in 1998 by the Marchetti family, Ristorante Imperiale was born from a single conviction: that authentic Italian cuisine, prepared with devotion and the finest ingredients flown from Emilia-Romagna, could transport a diner from the chaos of Mumbai to the quiet beauty of a Tuscan evening.

We source our truffles from Umbria, our San Marzano tomatoes from Campania, and our aged Parmigiano-Reggiano from the same consortium that has served us for two decades. Every element on your plate carries a provenance, a story, and an intention.

Il Maestro

Chef Marco Bellini

Executive Chef & Patron

Born in Bologna to a family of restaurateurs, Marco Bellini spent his formative years in the kitchen of his grandmother, learning that food is not a performance — it is a conversation between the cook, the land, and the diner.

Trained under Massimo Bottura at Osteria Francescana and later at Michel Bras in Laguiole, Chef Bellini returned to India in 2006 with a vision that has since become Ristorante Imperiale: Italian cooking in its most honest, elevated form, reimagined for the sensibility of the Indian palate without ever compromising the soul of the original.

His tasting menu — eight courses that take you from the Alps to the Sicilian coast — changes with each season. His philosophy is unyielding: the best ingredient, cooked with the least interference, presented with the utmost intention.

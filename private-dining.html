Sala Imperiale

Our Grandest Room

The Sala Imperiale is the jewel of Ristorante Imperiale — a high-ceilinged chamber dressed in bespoke Florentine wallpaper, hand-blown Murano chandeliers, and a marble-inlaid floor commissioned from a workshop in Carrara. It is a room that announces itself quietly and then overwhelms you.

Available for exclusive hire for dinner parties, wedding receptions, milestone celebrations, and product launches where the audience deserves more than a function room.

40 Max Guests
120 Sq. Meters
₹80K Min. Spend
  • Murano crystal chandeliers & bespoke Florentine wallpaper
  • Private butler & sommelier service
  • Custom floral arrangements by Casa di Fiori
  • AV system: 85" screen, ambient sound system
  • Personalised menus & hand-calligraphed place cards
  • Aperitivo reception area with cocktail service
  • Private entrance & cloakroom

Sala Toscana

Warmth & Intimacy

Named for the hills that inspired it, the Sala Toscana is lined with reclaimed terracotta brick, aged olive wood shelving, and a curated selection of ceramic artwork sourced from artisans in Montelupo Fiorentino. The room breathes warmth.

Perfect for family celebrations, rehearsal dinners, and intimate corporate lunches where conversation should flow as freely as the wine.

18 Max Guests
55 Sq. Meters
₹35K Min. Spend
  • Terracotta & olive wood interior, handmade ceramic art
  • Dedicated server & wine steward
  • Custom menu tasting consultation with Chef Bellini
  • Vintage Chianti cellar display wall
  • Personalised menus printed in-house
  • Soundproofed for complete privacy

Il Tavolo dello Chef

Inside the Kitchen

The rarest table in Mumbai. Positioned inside Chef Bellini's kitchen, this single table for two to six guests offers an unparalleled view of a professional kitchen at full cinematic flow — the fire, the precision, the choreography of a two-starred brigade.

Chef Bellini cooks your meal personally, presenting each course at the pass and conversing throughout. An experience reserved for those who have dined everywhere and seek something genuinely unrepeatable.

2–6 Guests Only
12 Courses
₹22K Per Person
  • 12-course bespoke menu, created the morning of your dinner
  • Chef Bellini cooks & presents each course personally
  • Full wine pairing by Sommelier Priya Malhotra
  • Kitchen tour & behind-the-scenes briefing
  • Signed menu card & kitchen photograph as keepsake
  • Available Tuesday–Saturday by prior arrangement only

The Journey

Eight Chapters of Italia

01 Benvenuto — Welcome

Amuse-Bouche della Tradizione

Emilia-Romagna & Piemonte

Three miniature bites — a Parmigiano Reggiano frico cup filled with 36-month prosciutto mousse; a Barolo-braised beef cromesqui; and a wafer of lardo di Colonnata draped over warm focaccina. An overture of the evening.

02 Antipasto

Carpaccio di Wagyu,
Tartufo Bianco d'Alba

Piemonte

Paper-thin sheets of A5 wagyu tenderloin, shaved Alban white truffle, green apple slivers, aged Aceto Balsamico Tradizionale di Modena DOP 25 years, mustard gelée, edible gold.

Wine Pairing Barolo Chinato Cocchi — Piedmont, NV

Sorbet di Limone Amalfitano — a pause between the land and the sea. Cold, bright, necessary.

03 Primo

Risotto Mantecato al
Tartufo Nero di Norcia

Umbria

Carnaroli riserva, lightly toasted in dry white wine, finished with 48-month Parmigiano and unsalted Isigny butter. Shaved fresh Norcia black truffle tableside. The dish that made Chef Bellini's reputation.

Wine Pairing Sagrantino di Montefalco 2017 — Arnaldo-Caprai
04 Pasta

Tagliolini al Caviale
Oscietra & Champagne

Veneto / The Caspian

Hand-rolled 00 flour tagliolini, simmered in champagne-butter, finished with sour cream, preserved lemon zest, chives, and a generous tableside crown of Oscietra Imperial caviar (30g per portion).

Wine Pairing Franciacorta Dosage Zéro Riserva 2018 — Ca' del Bosco

Granita di Sicilia al Gelsomino — jasmine ice, a whisper of Marsala, hand-candied cedro peel. The south interrupts the north, as it always does.

05 Secondo di Mare

Branzino alla Ligure,
Olive Taggiasche & Pinoli

Liguria

Wild-caught European sea bass, scaled and roasted en papillote with Ligurian Taggiasca olives, toasted pine nuts, cherry tomatoes, fresh oregano, and Ligurian DOP olive oil. Served with a saffron-potato crisp.

Wine Pairing Vermentino di Sardegna Superiore 2021 — Sella & Mosca
06 Secondo di Carne

Lombo di Vitello alla
Valdostana, Fonduta di Fontina

Valle d'Aosta

Rose veal loin, sous-vide 52°C for four hours, seared over beechwood, rested and crowned with warm Fontina Val d'Aosta fonduta. Wild mushroom sauté, black truffle jus, crispy sweetbread. The mountain in every bite.

Wine Pairing Barolo DOCG 2017 — Giacomo Conterno "Cascina Francia"
07 Formaggi d'Italia

Selezione di Formaggi
Affinati del Carrello

All Italy

The cheese trolley arrives — a rotating selection of five to seven Italian artisan cheeses: from Gorgonzola Naturale to Pecorino di Pienza to aged Grana Padano Riserva. Accompanied by chestnut honey, mostarda di Cremona, and grissini torinesi.

Wine Pairing Passito di Pantelleria "Ben Ryé" 2020 — Donnafugata
08 Dolce Finale

Soufflé al Cioccolato
Valrhona, Gelato al Fior di Sale

Sicily / Sorrento

A 70% Guanaja dark chocolate soufflé, baked precisely eight minutes, served at the table the instant it is ready. Alongside: a quenelle of house-churned Fleur de Sel gelato, preserved Amalfi lemon curd, and a scattering of Bronte pistachio praline.

Wine Pairing Marsala Superiore Riserva "Marco De Bartoli" — Florio, 10yr

Piccola Pasticceria & Caffè — the journey closes with housemade petit fours, a single espresso ristretto from our Neapolitan blend, and — should you wish — a glass of aged Grappa di Monovitigno.
